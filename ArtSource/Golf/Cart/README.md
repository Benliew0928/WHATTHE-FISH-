# Golf cart source and delivery

The user-supplied Meshy ZIP is retained unchanged in `Originals/`; its hash is in [provenance.json](provenance.json). No generation service or account was used. `GolfCart.blend` keeps the full 246,850-triangle editable mesh, source maps and three delivery LODs. Source texture dependencies are relative to this master. Each delivery LOD has a fixed body and four wheel meshes, totalling **33,958 / 13,805 / 6,721 triangles**. The delivery wheels follow the approved [side](References/Wheel-side.png) and [tread](References/Wheel-tread.png) references: thick rounded black tyres and small cream six-spoke rims, with geometric chevron channels. Loose dark fragments below both rear wheel wells are removed. Four fixed axles and upright mounts join the hubs to the chassis, adding only **288 / 224 / 160 triangles** to the three body LODs. Their ends overlap the hub and original chassis, including full front steering. The original body subset geometry, winding and UVs are retained alongside these support faces. The master contains 16 mesh objects: one unchanged high-quality source and 15 delivery parts. The wheels use the existing separate collision hull.

The active [Unity art](../../../Game/Assets/_Game/Art/Golf/Cart/) uses a 2.85 m length, centred ground pivot, front marker and driver-seat marker. All three wheel LODs share each wheel's axle pivot; the front pair steer and all four roll without moving the fixed fenders. Tyre radii remain 0.339 m front and 0.328 m rear, with 64/40/24 angular segments per LOD. **All four rim diameters are 0.38 m**, centred on the rolling axle. Their front/rear bead loops share vertices with the tyre rather than overlapping separate cut-off surfaces. The round lip, six narrow spokes, near-LOD bevels and broad central cap have a cream finish. Chevron channels are cut into the closed tyre shell, retaining a centred circular outer envelope; there are **16/10/6 rows** and a maximum **6 mm depth**. Mid/far wheels reduce axial tread samples and omit the small spoke bevels. New faces sample uniform rubber and cream regions of the existing atlas. Base colour and normal maps are unchanged at 1024²; the unchanged 512² mask packs metallic in red and smoothness in alpha. Unity uses ASTC 6×6 colour, 4×4 normal and 8×8 mask on Android, one shared instanced material and three distance LODs. Five renderers are active per cart LOD; no wheel texture or material is added. The original high-resolution ZIP and master remain outside Unity's asset folders.

From any working directory, run `Tools/Blender/prepare_golf_cart.py` with Blender's background Python, or a Python installation with `bpy` and NumPy. The default checkout root resolves from the script; `--root` selects a different checkout. The generator checks topology, resolves all delivery texture references, preserves non-path FBX payloads during format-aware rewriting and saves portable master dependencies. Extraction preserves authored winding on the open body subsets. The closed wheel components have outward normals and valid UVs on every bevel and cap. Continuous UV strips inside each swatch limit unnecessary imported vertex splits. Temporary extraction, renders and import evidence go under ignored `Builds/GolfCartQA/`.

The Chinese cart controls, Golf Aim/Cancel labels and desktop key hints share a 6,256-byte subset of Noto Sans SC. The unmodified font, its [SIL OFL licence](Fonts/OFL.txt) and download provenance are retained in `Fonts/`; the full licence also ships as a Unity resource beside the subset. Regenerate with `Tools/Build/Prepare-CartLabels.py` using Python with `fonttools`; the default root resolves from that script. The derived family is renamed **Golf Cart Controls**, preserves licence records and embeds the required glyphs for Android. TTF assets use Git LFS.

In Unity, **WHATTHE FISH? / Golf / Prepare golf cart** rebuilds the [resource prefab](../../../Game/Assets/_Game/Resources/GolfCart.prefab), material, mobile import settings and GolfCart collision layer. Normal player builds also call this preparation step; no scene regeneration or art downloads are needed.

The `GolfCart_Seat` marker in the master is the driver's hip target, at Blender coordinates **(-0.32, -0.20, 1.04)**. Edit that marker and the matching marker configuration in [prepare_golf_cart.py](../../../Tools/Blender/prepare_golf_cart.py) when changing the seat. [golf_cart_wheels.py](../../../Tools/Blender/golf_cart_wheels.py) holds axle centres, the shared `RIM_RADIUS`, `TREAD_ROWS`, `TREAD_DEPTH`, tyre/rim profiles, LOD segment counts and `add_axle_supports` dimensions; Unity's [GolfCartBuilder](../../../Game/Assets/_Game/Editor/GolfCartBuilder.cs) holds matching rolling radii. The visual rolling ratio defaults to **0.85** in [GolfCart.cs](../../../Game/Assets/_Game/Sports/Golf/GolfCart.cs), and appears as **Wheel Roll Scale** on the generated resource prefab. [audit_golf_cart.py](../../../Tools/Blender/audit_golf_cart.py) checks all twelve wheel orientations, tread envelope/groove depth/spacing, circular equal rims, welded bead edges, closed wheel surfaces, axle connections and portable active dependencies.
