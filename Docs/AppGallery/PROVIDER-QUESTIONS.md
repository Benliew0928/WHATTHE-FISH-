   and the stranger-chat social-app rule, apply to this sports game with optional
   room communication. We do not claim it is a child-only app.

We will disclose processing accurately and keep submission pending until the
required controls are implemented. Please identify the applicable current
AppGallery guidance and an acceptable method rather than assuming a vendor.
