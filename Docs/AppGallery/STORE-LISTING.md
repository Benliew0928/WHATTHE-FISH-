## Full description

Welcome to WHATTHE FISH?, a tropical multiplayer sports adventure.

Play golf, football, basketball and fishing across four islands. Gather your
friends in one room, travel together and compete for the best result.

On the golf island, aim your shot, drag the power slider and release to hit.
Explore the course in a golf buggy and adapt to changing events along the way.

Create a room and share its code, or use the Android QR scanner to join a friend's
room. Customize your player profile, add friends and send room invitations.
Optional room voice chat lets players talk while playing together.

Online multiplayer and voice features require an internet connection. Camera
access is requested for camera-based QR scanning. Microphone access is requested
for voice chat. Manual room-code entry remains available.

Owner must confirm feature availability in the final release before publication.
Do not advertise phone FPS, offline coverage of every mode, or player counts
until verified on the signed candidate.
