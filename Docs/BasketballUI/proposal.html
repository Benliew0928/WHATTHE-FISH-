Court guide

Get the ball

Walk close to a reachable loose ball to pick it up. Dribbling is automatic.

Make it count

Inside the arc: 2 points.
Beyond the arc: 3 points.
On the line counts as 2.

Stay in play

Block, guard or steal to contest the ball. Chase rebounds after a miss.

Shared practice score · No match timer or foul penalties

Move & look

Left stick to move; push fully to run. Drag open space on the right to look or aim.

Shoot & finish

Hold Shoot; release in green. Keep holding and follow the upper path for Dunk, diagonal path for Layup.

Pass your way

Tap short; hold for distance. Slide up for Loft, diagonally inward for Bounce. Lift your finger to pass.

Defend

Tap Block or swipe up for Jump Block. Hold Guard or Steal. Jump becomes Jump Block.

Lift in Cancel to stop a shot or pass; slide back to keep aiming. An unavailable finish uses a normal timed shot.

Lock-on Shot

Hold the ball and tap. Plant for 2 seconds, then shoot automatically.

A block or lost possession cancels it. The prop is spent.

Team Turbo

You and your teammates move at 1.5× speed for 5 seconds.

Jump height stays the same. Boosts do not stack.

Portal Steal

Use from your home half. Pull the ball to you, even in flight.

Cannot undo a basket or take a ball you already hold.

One slot · One use · New pickups replace the old prop. Use inside the painted court.

Leave the court?

You’re the host. Everyone will return to the room, and this practice score will reset.
