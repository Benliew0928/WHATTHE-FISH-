# F1 and B1 Palm Shore exteriors

> Historical milestone. The current Windows build replaces these original venue bounds and basketball roof with the walkable coastal design. See [current implementation](COASTAL-STADIUMS.md) and its actual EXE evidence.

The Windows game contains a separate sea island around each existing football and basketball venue. The approved Palm Shore concepts guide the low sandy coastline, palms, landscaped loop, rocks and arrival dock. The stadium buildings are retained as requested; the basketball roof remains in place for this exterior-only pass.

## Art and rendering

- Two continuous sculpted ground meshes, split into 16 sectors each, with 4096² terrain color maps and tiled detail relief.
- Rounded limestone clusters with a nonperiodic painted texture, moss patches, scattered pebbles and modeled shells.
- Curved palm trunks with individual bark rings, coconuts, frond ribs and paired leaflets. Layered flowers and short grass occupy the planted areas around the path.
- Timber arrival docks and small garden bridges with individual beveled planks, bearers, posts, bolt heads, caps, rope rails and modeled lashings.
- Animated water with a shallow-to-deep color transition, shoreline wash, near-camera glints and caustic variation. Small waves fade at distance to reduce shimmer.
- A blue gradient sky with a field of connected 3D cumulus meshes. Cloud positions and sizes vary around each island.

Desktop rendering uses a separate pipeline with full-resolution rendering, 4× MSAA, four shadow cascades, soft shadows and exterior contact shading. The original mobile pipeline asset remains separate. Contact shading is disabled inside either original venue so it does not affect court markings. Basketball keeps its original indoor shadow behavior on court and uses sunlight shadows for exterior cameras. The original bright display palette is retained for these two worlds.

The exterior art contains approximately 3.25 million source triangles for football and 2.28 million for basketball, before the existing venue and cloud instances. This is a Windows visual pass; exterior LOD optimization and Android device profiling are not included.

## Editable sources

- `ArtSource/CoastalIslands/PalmShore_Islands.blend` — both exterior collections, source geometry and packed surface textures. Basketball is hidden initially so the two origin-centered islands do not overlap in the viewport.
- `Game/Assets/_Game/Art/CoastalIslands/` — independent FBX scenery, PNG maps, material assets and water/sky/cloud shaders.
- `Tools/Blender/build_coastal_islands.py` — deterministic generator; it writes only the new coastal asset directories.
- `Game/Assets/_Game/Editor/CoastalIslandBuilder.cs` — imports materials and attaches each exterior under its existing venue.

The root name in Bootstrap is **Palm Shore Island - Exterior Only**. The outer world is grouped by terrain sectors, coastal rocks, palm gardens, meadow sectors, beach details, docks and bridge parts. The two stadium art prefabs and their source Blender files are not regenerated by the coastal generator.

## Verification

`ArtSource/CoastalIslands/venue-preservation-audit.json` compares SHA-256 hashes of both original venue Blender files and reusable art prefabs against the pre-change baseline. All four remain unchanged.

`ArtSource/CoastalIslands/blender-audit.json` verifies finite vertices, valid UV coordinates, upward terrain normals, 16 terrain sectors per island, FBX output presence, and the unchanged venue hashes.

`Docs/VisualDirection/CoastalIslands/review.txt` records runtime checks for both scenery roots, one active world, original seating LOD counts, all 20 original spawn floors, preserved basketball ceiling, sky assignment, absence of exterior collision, and switching among all four sports. The capture runner rejects shader errors, exceptions, missing rendering resources and inactive character-controller calls.

The final Windows executable also passed both existing sport smoke tests: offline movement and all player camera modes, plus basketball logo selection, palette customization, ceiling collision and wall-camera clearance. Reports are in `Builds/CoastalFinalQA/`. `Docs/VisualDirection/CoastalIslands/build-evidence.json` records the reviewed assembly/game-data hashes and capture count. No runtime exceptions or missing shader resources were found in the final review or smoke runs.

Rebuild the game with `Tools/Build/Build.ps1 -Target Windows`. The build registers the desktop renderer while compiling so Unity retains its contact-shading resources, then restores the project's original graphics settings. `Builds/WindowsFinal/LATEST-BUILD.txt` identifies the build time and included exteriors.
