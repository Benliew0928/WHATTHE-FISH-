# Golf cart gameplay

Golf exploration has one **召唤 / 收回** button for each player's own cart. Summoning places the supplied Meshy cart on nearby supported, unobstructed terrain; repeated requests cannot create a second cart for that player. A failed placement leaves the button on 召唤. Only the summoner can recall their cart. Recalling an occupied cart first exits its driver onto supported ground.

Near an empty cart, **驾驶** enters its driver's seat. Every player can drive another player's empty cart. One cart has one driver; a distant request or second driver is rejected. While seated, the same interaction shows **离开**. Leaving parks the cart without deleting it. The left joystick drives forward/reverse and steers; releasing the stick brakes. Look dragging and all three camera modes remain available. Desktop controls are **R** for summon/recall and **E** for drive/leave.

First-person, third-person and elevated cameras follow the cart's actual heading change one-to-one, preserving any manual look offset. Steering left turns the elevated view left; steering right turns it right, using the same follow path as third-person. Its existing 58° downward angle and 10 m distance remain. Third-person reverse driving smoothly turns the view 180° towards the direction of travel; forward input smoothly removes that reverse offset. First-person and elevated reverse driving keep their forward-facing offset. Releasing the stick keeps the last travel-facing direction until forward/reverse input changes it. Camera updates run after the displayed cart pose is interpolated, so driving clients follow the same turn as their seat. Leaving the cart or changing islands clears camera follow/reverse state without changing walking cameras.

`PlayerView` exposes **Cart Reverse Turn Time** (0.45 s smoothing) and **Cart Reverse Turn Speed** (180°/s maximum) in the Inspector. These tune the third-person transition independently of vehicle speed, steering and networking; 0.45 s is the smoothing parameter, not the total duration of a 180° turn. No camera state is replicated.

Cart controls are available only during Golf exploration. Football, Basketball, Fishing, menus and shared travel cannot summon or drive carts. Leaving Golf parks current drivers before shared travel and hides stored carts; returning to Golf restores their parked positions. Ending a player's session removes that player's cart and safely releases another player who was driving it. Existing sport kick/shoot tuning and ball physics are unchanged.

Forward and reverse limits are **17 m/s** and **8 m/s**, exactly 2× the original 8.5/4 m/s. Acceleration and braking are doubled with them, retaining the original time to accelerate or stop. Maximum steering yaw is **120°/s**, increased from 72°/s, with **38°** front-wheel steering instead of 28°. These values are together in `GolfCartMotor`. Throttle uses stick magnitude and forward/reverse intent separately from steering: a fully pushed diagonal stick retains 17 m/s forward or 8 m/s reverse; half-strength input retains the same half-speed on a turn as straight ahead. Releasing the stick brakes; side-only input retains stationary steering.

Four independent wheel pivots roll from actual displayed ground travel, including reverse and different wheel paths through a turn; only the front pair steer. Body pitch, roll and vertical settling cannot spin a stationary wheel. Stationary or blocked carts do not keep spinning their wheels, and a summon/rebase does not produce a rotation burst. **Wheel Roll Scale = 0.85** reduces visual rotation by 15%; this is an intentional presentation calibration, with **1.0** giving the tyre-circumference ratio. Tune the resource prefab's `GolfCart` component under **Wheel presentation** for preview; change the default in [GolfCart.cs](../Game/Assets/_Game/Sports/Golf/GolfCart.cs) to retain the setting when the builder regenerates that prefab. Wheel radii and pivots remain exposed there. A development probe verifies 10/25/50 cm starts, reverse movement, stationary target speed and body settling against the rendered tyre radii.

Delivery wheels follow the user's [side](../ArtSource/Golf/Cart/References/Wheel-side.png) and [tread](../ArtSource/Golf/Cart/References/Wheel-tread.png) references: thick rounded black tyre walls, geometric chevron channels and cream six-spoke rims. All four rims use the same **0.38 m diameter**, concentric with the rolling axle, and share front/rear bead edges with the tyre. Channels are recessed into one closed shell rather than floating tread pieces. Tyre radii remain **0.339 m front / 0.328 m rear**, preserving ground clearance and rolling calibration. The three LODs use **16/10/6 tread rows**, with fewer axial profile samples at distance. Each hub connects to the chassis through a fixed axle and upright mount. They extend inside the hub, including full front steering, and merge into each body LOD without adding renderers or colliders. Loose dark fragments below both rear wheel wells are removed. The same unchanged atlas and material serve every wheel and mount; no new texture is shipped.

Sand-bowl rims use the motor's four-wheel ground support, slope and height checks. Body sweeps exclude the terrain meshes: their initial overlap on an uphill lip can report a false horizontal wall and stop a supported cart. Solid props, other carts, shoreline boundaries and unsupported/steep ground retain their existing checks. The development cart probe crosses all five real sand bowls in four directions, both forwards and in reverse.

The driver's knees remain **straight** while seated. Both thigh and shin segments align forward and slightly upward, using their existing lengths; the shoes stay ahead of and above the seat cushion. Both feet align their actual toe-tip segments with the cart front, keeping the soles level instead of pointing downward. The ankle-to-toe-base segment slopes naturally and is not used as the shoe facing axis. The existing seated hip and steering-wheel hand targets remain. The hip marker is **1.04 m** high, leaving roughly **5.8 cm** beneath the roof collider. Walking and jump poses resume on exit. Tune the seat marker in [the source guide](../ArtSource/Golf/Cart/README.md) and the leg direction in [GolfCartDriverPose.cs](../Game/Assets/_Game/Sports/Golf/GolfCartDriverPose.cs).

The host validates owner actions and simulates driving. Replicated cart state lives on the summoner's network player, including cart pose, speed, front-wheel steering angle, driver ID and revision. Clients interpolate presentation and seat the appropriate avatar; they cannot set a cart pose or claim recall ownership. Wheel animation follows that presentation without adding another physics simulation or network manager. Reliable action edges are independent of movement throttling. Matching host/client builds are required; the current protocol is defined in [RoomService.cs](../Game/Assets/_Game/Shared/Networking/RoomService.cs). Four islands, one active room game and group travel are preserved.

[Art sources and regeneration](../ArtSource/Golf/Cart/README.md) retain the high-quality master outside shipped assets. [Test-GolfCart.ps1](../Tools/Build/Test-GolfCart.ps1) checks real buttons, driving, collisions, parking, owner restrictions and two-player loopback replication in a development Windows player. Current raw evidence stays under ignored `Builds/GolfCartQA/`; Android measurements and validation scope are recorded after the release build in [BUILD-SIZE.md](BUILD-SIZE.md) and [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

## Initial summon and driving validation

The original summon/recall and public empty-cart driving implementation passed **76 offline/host/client assertions**, along with football, basketball, shared jump, golf traversal and group-travel regressions. Its protocol 18 base APK is retained at `Builds/GolfCartQA/ReleaseBase/WhatTheFish-release.apk`, measuring **88,756,434 bytes**, with **11,243,566 bytes** hard-limit headroom. The protocol 19 speed/wheel changes described above and concurrent course refinement are later work; they are outside that base snapshot's validation. See [the exact snapshot scope](VERIFICATION.md#golf-cart-summon-and-driving-snapshot--3-october-2026).
