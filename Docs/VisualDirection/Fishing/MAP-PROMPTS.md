# Map-only concept prompts

Built-in imagegen edits of the earlier fishing mockups. Each corresponding source image is the edit target and style reference. These prompts remove interface and characters and recompose the environment as a complete island overview.

## Map 1

Use case: stylized-concept.
Edit target: the supplied fishing concept image. Transform it into a MAP-ONLY environment design reference for building a Blender 3D game level.
Keep the established 3D cartoon art direction: chunky rounded limestone, soft sculpted palms and bushes, broad painted surfaces, cream beaches, lime green turf, sparse oversized coral/yellow/violet flowers, layered turquoise-to-blue ocean, soft sunny lighting and colored bounce. Same game world and material language as the source.
Change camera to a high aerial three-quarter orthographic-like overview from the south, looking north; show the ENTIRE island surrounded by a generous continuous border of ocean on every side. Landscape 16:9, one island in one single image. Clearly readable terrain and paths, not a close-up. Terrain is grounded in water, not a floating cutaway. Island fills about 75% of the image; no distant giant landmass. Make it look like a polished environment-only Blender beauty render with modelable geometry, restrained details.
REMOVE ALL interface completely: no buttons, joystick, timer, counters, scoreboards, panels, minimap, floating icons, glowing rings, labels, arrows, text, lettering, logos or watermarks anywhere. REMOVE all people, player characters, fishing rods and fishing lines. No billboard signage. No physical lettered signs. Fishing function should be communicated through beaches and timber piers only.
Keep walkable surfaces continuous and visibly connected. Gentle paths, clear casting areas at water edges. Timber piers must attach visibly to land and have unobstructed outer fishing edges. No barriers across casting faces. No golf holes or flags.

OPTION 1 — SHORELINE ISLAND. A single broad low bean-shaped tropical island approximately 140 by 105 metres, dominant open grassy center with a low soft hill near the north. Its south edge forms one broad shallow crescent beach bay, west and east shore curve gently into smaller beach pockets. Three compact attached timber fishing piers at west, east and north shores; the south shore stays a wide open sandy fishing beach. A continuous cream walking loop links all shores, with two gentle diagonal shortcuts across the green center. A few rounded limestone clusters at the northern headland and low scattered rock accents elsewhere, sparse palms and flower clumps leave central terrain legible. Most shoreline stays low and accessible. Keep this silhouette SIMPLE, OPEN and LOW compared with a ring lagoon or cliff island. No waterfall or bridge. 3 timber piers total. All 3 piers visible.
