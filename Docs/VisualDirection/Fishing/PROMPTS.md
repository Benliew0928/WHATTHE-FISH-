## Concept 1

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity landscape 16:9 mobile multiplayer fishing game gameplay screen, one full-bleed screen, no device frame or presentation board.
Primary request: explore a new fishing sport for the existing cartoon sports game. Players compete by NUMBER OF FISH caught before the match timer expires. Every fish adds exactly one; no money, rarity points, weight score, battle pass or inventory shop.
Visual direction: A HIGH QUALITY 3D CARTOON ANIMATED WORLD, deliberately far from reality. Rounded chunky gently asymmetrical sculpted forms, broad painted color variation, sparse oversized grass tufts and flowers, soft daylight with colored bounce, clean shadows. Island in the middle of the sea, cream sand, layered cyan and turquoise water, lime-green grass, sculpted palms, rounded pale stone cliffs, distant sea stacks and cloud puffs. Coordinated teal/cyan/coral/yellow/violet accents. Main avatar has large head, compact body, short springy limbs, expressive simple face, sculpted teal hair with coral/yellow tufts, oversized colorful sneakers; fishing outfit. Other players use same stylization with distinct outfit colors.
UI: polished rounded cream panels, deep teal readable rounded sans-serif text, chunky colorful controls, restrained soft shadows. Landscape mobile safe margins, clear hierarchy, large touch controls with generous spacing. Keep playfield and fishing line readable. Actual gameplay HUD, not a website. Exact short English text specified below; no additional long text.
Avoid: photorealism, flat vector illustration, realistic humans, gritty materials, tiny text, clutter, huge decorative title, golf clubs/holes/flags, brand logos, watermarks.
CONCEPT 1: SHORELINE SPRINT. The welcoming free-roaming third-person option, closest to the golf island mood. Camera behind the main athlete, standing on a curved cream beach beside a short timber pier; see an expansive open cyan bay and the lush island coast curving around the right, distant sea stacks beyond. Two other competitors fish from separated beaches and pier farther away. Main avatar in lower middle, rod line visibly reaching a bobber in open water. Small dotted casting arc toward a subtle target ripple. Daylight, turquoise dominant, lush flowers and palms, large environment takes at least 75% of screen.

Bottom left translucent movement joystick. Bottom center small cream pill with fish icon "10 CAUGHT". Bottom right large teal round "CAST" button, separate smaller cream "BAG" button above. Pause icon near timer. Casting state only, no contradictory reel or catch popup. Professional clean HUD composition.
