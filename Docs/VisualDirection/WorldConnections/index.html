CONCEPT 01

Beacon Bay

Natural / familiar

Harbor landmarks + sea lanes

A lighthouse gives the world one shared point of orientation. Lantern jetties and curved buoy lanes make the islands feel like one welcoming resort.

Best fit for a calm, cohesive coastal resort.

The central lighthouse is scenery, not a required transfer hub.

CONCEPT 02

Ribbon Reef

Natural / expressive

Submerged reefs + currents

Turquoise currents and submerged reef ribbons braid through open sea. Crescent decks and pearl-shell landmarks mark the island arrival coves.

My preferred starting point for the existing tropical world.

At eye height, island silhouettes do more work than the underwater pattern.

CONCEPT 03

Sky-Sail Circuit

Playful / active

Gondolas + shore stations

Fine cables and colorful gondolas link matching sail-canopy stations. A visible aerial circuit gives the archipelago a playful transport identity.

Best for a lively ocean adventure park.

The travel motif can support a future island switch; continuous rides are not assumed.

CONCEPT 04

Tidegate Ruins

Fantasy / architectural

Tide gates + sea ruins

Carved shore gates and broken submerged ruins suggest an old sea sanctuary. Each island feels connected through a shared magical history.

Best if teleportation should belong visibly to the setting.

Keep the ruins sparse and distinguish crafted gates from golf’s natural sea arch.

CONCEPT 05

Starfall Sea

Fantasy / atmospheric

Constellations + astrolabes

Brass celestial markers and delicate trails of starlight turn the four islands into a constellation. Reflections unite the sea at blue hour.

Best for a distinctive, dreamlike world.

This dusk treatment needs a daylight interpretation before selection is finalized.

THE SHARED DESIGN RULE

The horizon should tell you where you could go.

  • Retain football’s scalloped roof, basketball’s open bowl, golf’s sea arch and waterfall, and fishing’s palm-and-limestone silhouette.
  • Give every island a shore lookout toward the other three, with clear water gaps and consistent geographical bearings.
  • Fishing’s lagoon circle is clearest from above. Its outward silhouette needs care so it is also recognizable from another island.
  • Future distant island replacements should preserve those signatures. Rendering, transitions and animation remain for a later pass.
